Short Words for Common Things

Best Guess

A Kalman filter (Kálmán, 1960 — flying on Apollo by 1966) tracks a target through a noisy sensor: new belief = old belief + gain × surprise, the gain recomputed each step from its own uncertainty. The violet ellipse is that uncertainty, breathing as the signal comes and goes. Drag the target to feel the lag; wind down assumed agility for a smooth, hopeless sceptic.

The gain answers a question that isn't about spacecraft: how far should new evidence move me? Never a personality trait — a ratio of how confident I am against how noisy the source is, kept explicit and up to date.
